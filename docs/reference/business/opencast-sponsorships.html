opencast Reference: sponsorships, both sides
Biz 04Opencast for business and master control, web and phone

Support a station, and be thanked on air.

Sponsorship is underwriting: a business supports a whole station or one program for a flat amount each month, and is named in the station's thank-you credit. No spot, no sales pitch, no rate per viewer. This file shows both sides: the business choosing what to sponsor and writing a credit that passes the rules, and the station approving it in master control.

Who you are, not what you sell

A credit names the business, where it is and what it does. No prices, offers, comparisons or calls to action. The rules are checked as you type.

The station says yes

Nothing is credited on a station that didn't approve it. The station sets a minimum per program and decides who it thanks.

Made for them

Nobody designs a credit. Opencast generates it in the station's colour, and it updates when sponsors change.

Paid a month at a time

Held from the balance at the start of each month, paid to the station at the end, like spots are paid when they air.

A spot

Your own :30 ad, with offers and a code. You pay each time it airs, and stations choose when.

A sponsorship

A thank-you credit the station airs in its breaks and lists on its page. One flat amount a month; no offers, no code.

Monthly, not per airing

Sponsorships show a monthly amount and a start date. There's no budget bar, because there's nothing to use up.

The difference, stated once

Two short lines under the list so a business can tell which it wants without reading a help page.

Fix the 2 flagged phrases to send.

The rules, as suggestions

Each flagged phrase says what the problem is and offers a fix. The credit can't be sent until both are resolved, so a station never has to reject one for wording.

The preview already applies the fix

The slate shows the credit as it would read after "Use that", so the business sees the acceptable version, not its draft.

Room is shown

"No sponsors yet, room for 2." Stations cap sponsors per program, so a credit never becomes a list nobody reads.

Held monthly

The month's amount is held on the 1st and paid to the station at the end. If the balance can't cover it, the sponsorship lapses at the end of its last paid month and the credit drops out.

05The credit on air

Ten seconds in the station's colour.

The generated credit as it airs in Beat Tape Live's first break on October 3, full screen, before the station ID. It's regenerated automatically whenever a sponsor starts, lapses or changes its line.

Set in the house type

Archivo for names, Public Sans for the line, inside title safe, in the station's colour. Every station's credits look like that station, and all of them look like Opencast.

Ten seconds, read or not

A credit runs :10 to :15, as the style guide says for underwriting. A station can have it read aloud by its station ID voice, or leave it as text over its bumper music.

It appears on the page too

Under "Made possible by" on BEAT's station page, as the viewer app file showed.
