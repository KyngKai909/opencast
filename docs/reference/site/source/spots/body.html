How the breaks stay goodFor everyone
  1. 01
    Checked before it airs

    Length, loudness and picture, so it airs cleanly on every station. Your code and QR go on the end card.

  2. 02
    Stations choose

    Each station decides which spots air in its breaks, and can turn away whole categories. Your spot only airs where it's welcome.

  3. 03
    Counted, never guessed

    Viewers on the dial come from our own player. Relay viewers come from YouTube and Twitch, and count only when the platform says where they are.

  4. 04
    Pay only for what aired

    Each airing settles at what it really cost. Airings cut short come back in full. Anything unspent stays yours to use or withdraw.

  5. 05
    Stop any time

    Pause a spot and stations fill its time. Bring it back when you like. Ending a sponsorship stops the renewal; it runs to the end of the month you paid for.

Questions advertisers ask7 answers
Do I have to be local?

No. Shops set a distance from their address. Online businesses pick the markets they ship to, and codes used at checkout count on their own.

How much should I spend?

Whatever you like: there's no minimum and no contract. Set a budget and a most-per-day, and the planner shows how long it lasts. You can change both at any time.

What rate should I set?

A rate per 1,000 tuned in means you pay for viewers, whatever the station. A rate per airing suits a small station you want to be on. Higher rates get picked by more stations.

What if nobody watches?

Then that airing costs nothing. What was held for it comes back to your balance a few minutes after it ends.

Can I choose the stations?

You choose the area, the kinds of station and the times of day; stations choose the spots. To be on one station or one show for sure, sponsor it.

Do I need a spot already?

No. Order one made by a station that makes spots, or by the network's studio. They quote before anything is paid, and the finished spot is yours.

Where do I see results?

Every airing, with the station, the time, how many tuned in and what it cost. Customers from your code, and what each one cost. A statement every month.
