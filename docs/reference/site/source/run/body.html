06Questions

Questions
operators ask.

Before you switch7 answers
Do I need a GPU or a server?

No. Files are prepared once on our side. The library agent only copies files and runs on any NAS, Mac, PC or Raspberry Pi.

Will my YouTube or Twitch stream drop?

No. Platforms see one continuous stream, even when a live block starts or ends. Where a platform caps stream length, like Twitch at 48 hours, we restart ahead of it at a break.

Can I bring my ErsatzTV or Tunarr channels?

Yes. Bring your collections, bumpers and blocks; they become schedule blocks and break rules. Your channel shows up on the dial and still works in Plex.

Who buys the spots in my breaks?

Anyone with something to sell: a shop near your viewers, an online store, an app. They buy in the spot market and you approve what airs. Local ads are priced on viewers we can place nearby; online advertisers pay for every viewer. If you turn on backfill, unsold time goes to the network's video ad partners.

What about music and film rights?

Every program has a rights record. If someone files a claim, that program comes off the air everywhere at once while it's sorted out, and your channel keeps running.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your channel stays on the dial. After a 14-day grace period, paid relays and live hours pause until the account is topped up.

Can I leave?

Yes. The software is open source, and you can export your schedule and library list at any time.
