No account to tune in, on a phone, a TV or a browser.

Spots, sponsors and members' pledges go to the station that aired them.

Shows aired with a creator's permission earn from day one. The money waits in escrow.

A claim takes a program off the air everywhere at once while it's sorted out.

Anyone can see how the dial works and help make it better.
